# Data availability

Place the corresponding archived inputs under `data/` if you have authorized access.

| File under `data/` | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `incarceration_counts.csv` | Archived incarceration series for Table 2, the conventional OLS diagnostic, and panel construction |
| `arrest_rates.csv` | Archived arrests series for Table 2 and panel construction |
| `corrections_spending.csv` | Archived corrections spending series for Table 2 and panel construction |
| `States_3_Strikes.xlsx` | State-year scaffold and policy indicators used for panel construction |

The paper attributes incarceration and arrest inputs to ICPSR and expenditure inputs to NASBO. The exact study releases, redistribution terms, and transformations that produced the archived intermediates remain unverified. To obtain the expected intermediates and policy scaffold, contact the author through the associated manuscript record. Check access terms through the original providers before obtaining or sharing source files.

## Manuscript assets and redistribution

The manuscript additionally needs the six published PNGs under `data/published-figures/` listed in [archive correspondence](docs/archive-correspondence.md). These are not bundled while asset rights are checked. The [ICPSR redistribution policy](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/sites/icpsr/about/policies/redistribution) applies to ICPSR-sourced data. Confirm permission for intermediate-data redistribution and the NASBO release and reuse terms.
